Drizzle Dash

A Getting Over It-inspired platformer about a fallen raindrop trying to return to the clouds by dashing between falling droplets.

Core design

The game centers on a single movement idea: using precise dashes to traverse upward between falling raindrops. Birds and lightning introduce additional traversal hazards. The intended experience is a challenging climb where timing and movement execution matter.

My contributions

  • Led a three-person team through a 48-hour game jam.
  • Developed player movement, physics, and input systems.
  • Coordinated development and recruited playtesters.

Design takeaway

The project demonstrates the value of building a game around a focused mechanic, especially under a short development deadline. Responsive movement and readable traversal challenges are central to making a skill-based platformer engaging.

Created with Jason Jang and Tenny George.
