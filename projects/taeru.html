Concept & inspiration

Taeru began as my final project for an Introduction to Japan course. I wanted to explore the civilian experience of wartime Japan through interactive storytelling, with an emphasis on the emotional experience rather than mechanical complexity.

Narrative design

Unlike my more mechanically focused projects, Taeru puts the story at the center of the experience. The design goal was to use the interactive medium to communicate a historical subject on a personal level, while keeping the scope small enough to develop a complete game independently.

My contributions

  • Designed the narrative and overall player experience.
  • Developed the entire game independently in Python.
  • Managed the project's scope and completed development in under one month while balancing coursework.
  • Packaged and released the game for Windows and macOS.

Design takeaway

As my first original game, Taeru represents an exploration of narrative-driven design and end-to-end development. It demonstrates how a focused concept can support a complete, emotionally driven player experience without requiring a large set of gameplay systems.
