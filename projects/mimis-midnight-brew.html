Mimi’s Midnight Brew

A memory-based arcade game where players help a witch cat brew potions under time pressure. I co-designed the gameplay and was the sole programmer.

Core gameplay

Players memorize a recipe, pop bubbles to collect ingredients in the correct order, and try to finish before time runs out. Each correct ingredient returns a little time, while mistakes cost time. Difficulty increases as players progress.

Memorize recipe→Choose ingredients→Complete order→Next round

Designing risk and reward

Recipe recall

Players can look at the recipe again, but doing so costs time. Information is useful, yet never completely free.

Ingredient choices

Wildcards add flexibility; banned ingredients and skulls make some choices especially risky.

Time economy

Correct ingredients refund a little time while errors reduce it, linking accuracy to survival.

Escalating challenge

Increasing difficulty makes the same core loop more demanding over successive rounds.

Scope and delivery

Our team built the game in 24 hours. I implemented the gameplay, scoring, recipe validation, time management, and difficulty progression, and published builds for web, macOS, and Windows.

Art by Sophia Li and Joanne Chen. The playable build and full credits are available on the game page.
